About Us

Local, reliable, and passionate about every car we touch.

NextDoor.
Next Level.

NextDoorCustoms started with a simple idea: your neighbor shouldn't have to drive across town to get their car looking its best. We bring professional-grade detailing straight to your driveway — or you can drop it off at our shop by appointment.

Based in the 203, we serve our local community and surrounding Connecticut towns with the same care we'd give our own vehicles. Every wash, wax, and detail is done by hand using quality products that protect your paint and leave your interior spotless.

We're not a car wash — we're detailers. That means we take our time, pay attention to the details, and don't hand your car back until it's exactly where it should be.
